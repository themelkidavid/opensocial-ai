# Innovation Discovery Engine

## Purpose

The Innovation Discovery Engine is a core component of OpenSocial AI.

Its purpose is to help community organisations and social-sector federations move beyond describing problems and begin systematically exploring possible new solutions.

The engine combines historical data, programme knowledge, research evidence, field experience and human judgement to generate **testable solution hypotheses**.

It does not present AI-generated ideas as established facts.

---

## The Core Question

Traditional programme analysis often asks:

> What happened?

The Innovation Discovery Engine goes further:

> Why did it happen?

> What are we missing?

> What patterns have we overlooked?

> What assumptions are we making?

> What has worked elsewhere?

> What could we try that we have not considered before?

---

## Input

The engine can work with multiple forms of information, including:

- Programme data
- Monitoring and evaluation information
- Historical reports
- Community feedback
- Field observations
- Organisational knowledge
- Research evidence
- Existing interventions
- Programme failures and challenges
- Cross-sector examples

Sensitive personal information should not be used unnecessarily.

Where possible, development and testing should use anonymised, aggregated or synthetic information.

---

# Discovery Process

The engine follows a structured sequence.

## Step 1: Define the Problem

Clearly describe:

- The problem
- Who is affected
- Where it occurs
- How long it has existed
- What has already been attempted
- What outcomes are currently observed

The engine should challenge vague problem definitions.

For example:

> "Young people are not accessing services."

should lead to further questions:

- Which young people?
- Which services?
- In which locations?
- At what stage do they drop out?
- Has this changed over time?
- What barriers have already been identified?

---

## Step 2: Analyse Historical Evidence

Examine available historical information for:

- Trends
- Patterns
- Changes over time
- Geographic differences
- Population differences
- Programme differences
- Unexpected results
- Recurring failures
- Positive outliers

The objective is to identify information that may not be obvious through conventional review.

---

## Step 3: Identify Gaps

The engine should identify what is known and what remains uncertain.

Examples:

```text
Known
  ↓
Observed pattern
  ↓
Possible explanation
  ↓
Evidence missing
  ↓
Question requiring investigation
```
